# Security

The Open3D apps are installed by the Open3D Launcher on people's computers, so a security problem in one
of them matters to everyone who uses it. Thank you for reporting it.

## Report a problem privately

Do not open a public issue. On the repository of the app, open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a
vulnerability**. Only the maintainers see the report.

Say, as far as you can:

- the app and its version, and Windows' and 3ds Max's versions if they matter;
- what someone could do, and what they need for it (a file they send, access to the network, ...);
- the steps to see it happen.

## What happens next

The maintainers answer in the report, fix the problem with a test that fails without the fix, and publish
a new version. The launcher then offers it to everyone. Once it is out, the report can be made public, with
credit to you if you want it.

## What counts

Anything that lets a file, a web page, another computer or another person make an Open3D app do what its
user did not ask for: run code, read or change files, reach the network, take a key, or get past a question
the app should ask. Problems in 3ds Max, Windows or other programs themselves go to their makers.
