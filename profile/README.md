# Open3D

Free, open-source tools for 3D artists who use 3ds Max. No accounts, no licence keys, no cloud: install
them and use them.

**Get them with the [Open3D Launcher](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/Open3D-Launcher/releases/latest)**: it installs,
updates and removes every app, on Windows 10 and 11.

## The apps

Programs of their own:

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| [OpenView](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/OpenView) | 360 panoramas and photos at full resolution |
| [OpenRef](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/OpenRef) | Reference-image boards that float over your work |
| [OpenFarm](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/OpenFarm) | A render farm for your own computers |
| [OpenLibrary](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/OpenLibrary) | Your 3D library, dragged into 3ds Max |

Plug-ins for 3ds Max:

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| [OpenTools](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/OpenTools) | Tool hub for 3ds Max |
| [OpenManager](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/OpenManager) | Scene setups and batch rendering for 3ds Max |
| [OpenScatter](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/OpenScatter) | Scatter millions of instances in 3ds Max |

Every app works on its own, and they share one way of being installed, set up and kept up to date. The
programs speak English and Spanish; the 3ds Max plug-ins, English.

## Make an app with us

New apps start from [Open3D-SDK](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/Open3D-SDK): the guidelines every Open3D
app follows (development, security, distribution and use) and a starter for each kind of app. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

Found a security problem? Report it privately: [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/Open3D-Suite/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
